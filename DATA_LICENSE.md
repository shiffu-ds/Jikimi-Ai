# Data license

Datasets released by this project (PakRoads, PakSnatch, RUVR and their "-mini" / "-sim"
variants) are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC 4.0)**, unless a dataset's datasheet says otherwise. Confirm the final choice with the
supervisor / university policy before the first public release (plan Section 9.6).

Conditions that apply to every release, whatever the license:

- faces and heads are blurred; no face templates, no identity labels
- every clip has written consent ids (`consent_ids` in `labels.jsonl`); no minors
- **not for**: identifying or tracking individuals, face recognition, demographic inference,
  surveillance of protests, journalists or political gatherings, employee productivity tracking
- withdrawal requests are honoured in the next release
