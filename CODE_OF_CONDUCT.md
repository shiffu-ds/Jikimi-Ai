# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Be respectful, assume good intent, and remember that the people in our footage deserve the same
respect as the people in our team: neutral language in alerts ("mashkook harkat", never "chor"),
no identity guessing, decisions by humans.

Report problems to the maintainers privately; reports are handled confidentially.
